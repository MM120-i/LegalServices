# Lexora website — Phase 1 content draft

Prepared October 23, 2026. Status: draft for Mahim and client review; no client approval recorded.

## Scope of this delivery

Website copy, service-page drafts, FAQs, metadata, contact/form messages, legal-page drafts, and a review checklist. The project root now includes the authorized Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind initialization plus a generated typed content catalog. No website design implementation, image generation, integrations, or deployment is included. Stop after Phase 1 until Mahim authorizes the next phase.

## Decisions agreed with Mahim

- Modern, approachable, professional, and easy to use.
- Colour palette is open; navy/gold is optional. Visual design belongs to the next phase.
- Shorter homepage: hero → services → about/trust → how to start → reviews → FAQs → location/contact.
- Clear, useful service copy rather than a compulsory 600-word minimum.
- Dummy content may fill missing information in private drafts/previews. Label it visibly and replace or remove before publication.
- Use “Request a Consultation” until the consultation model is confirmed.

The supplied build brief is reference material. Its coding-agent instructions have not been treated as authorization to build the site or advance beyond Phase 1. These agreed changes supersede conflicting directions in that brief.

## Editorial notation

- `[CONFIRM: …]` marks a business fact or proposed service promise needing client confirmation.
- `[PLACEHOLDER: …]` marks missing copy or an asset.
- `SAMPLE — NOT A REAL CLIENT REVIEW` must accompany each dummy testimonial wherever used.
- Editorial notes and source notes are not visitor-facing copy.

Business name, principal, credentials, address, and phone numbers are transcribed from the supplied brief, which attributes them to the business card. They have not been independently verified. Proposed descriptions of how Lexora works are draft commitments, not evidence of its existing procedures. The client should approve them together with the actual services offered.

All internal route paths are proposed destinations for a future website; no website routes exist yet. Legal and privacy copy needs a final check against the actual practice and eventual website providers.
