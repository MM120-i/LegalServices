## Disclaimer

Route: `/disclaimer`

H1: Website Disclaimer

### General information

This website provides general information about Abed Legal Services Professional Corporation and the types of matters the practice may accept. It is not legal advice and should not be relied on to decide what to do in a particular matter. Laws, procedures, and individual circumstances can change the appropriate response.

### No automatic client relationship

Visiting this website, calling, or submitting an inquiry does not by itself create a paralegal–client relationship. Abed must confirm that it can accept your matter, and the scope and terms of representation must be agreed before the practice undertakes work for you.

### Deadlines

Limitation periods, filing dates, and other deadlines may apply. Do not delay seeking advice. An inquiry, automated confirmation, or request for an appointment does not mean Abed will file documents, attend a hearing, or protect a deadline on your behalf.

### Professional scope and credentials

Ontario services are subject to the permitted scope of a licensed paralegal and the services the practice accepts. Sharf Uddin Abead’s Advocate | Supreme Court of Bangladesh credential does not indicate that he is licensed as a lawyer in Ontario.

### No outcome guarantees

No result is guaranteed. If genuine client reviews are published, they describe individual experiences and do not establish what will happen in another matter.

### Information sent through the website

Do not send highly sensitive or detailed confidential records with an initial inquiry. Ask the office how to share documents. See the Privacy Policy for information about website data handling.

### External links

Links to courts, tribunals, maps, reviews, or other websites are provided for convenience. Those services have their own content and policies. Contact the relevant organization if you need to verify its current instructions.
