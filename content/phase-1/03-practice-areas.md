# Practice Areas

Route: `/practice-areas`

H1: Paralegal services in Scarborough, Toronto.

Start with the issue you are facing. Abed Legal Services Professional Corporation lists six practice areas, along with notary public services. Each page explains what to ask about and what information may help at an initial conversation.

## Traffic Tickets & Provincial Offences

A ticket or summons can raise questions about your driving record, costs, and court process. Review your options before deciding how to respond.

Link: `/practice-areas/traffic-tickets-provincial-offences`

## Accident Benefits Claims

After a motor vehicle accident, your policy, medical information, and the insurer’s decisions can all matter. Ask about help with benefit documents and disputes.

Link: `/practice-areas/accident-benefits-claims`

## Refugee Hearings & Appeals

Preparing for an Immigration and Refugee Board proceeding can involve important evidence and strict deadlines. Ask about assistance within permitted paralegal scope.

Link: `/practice-areas/refugee-hearings-appeals`

## Landlord & Tenant Matters

Understand a rental notice, application, or hearing process before deciding your next step. Whether you are a landlord or tenant, confirm whether Abed accepts your type of matter.

Link: `/practice-areas/landlord-tenant-matters`

## Employment & Human Rights Matters

A dismissal, unpaid wages, and discrimination may involve different legal processes. Start by identifying the issue and the forum that can address it.

Link: `/practice-areas/employment-human-rights-matters`

## Small Claims Court Matters

Get oriented before making or defending a claim for money or personal property. Ask about pleadings, evidence, settlement, and hearing preparation.

Link: `/practice-areas/small-claims-court-matters`

## Notary Public & Commissioning

Confirm what your receiving organization requires, then call to ask about document services and appointment availability.

Link: `/notary-public`

## Not sure where your matter fits?

You do not need to identify the legal category before calling. Give a short description and mention any upcoming date. Abed can discuss whether the matter falls within the services it accepts.
