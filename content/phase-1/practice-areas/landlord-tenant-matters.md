## Understand the document before reacting

A landlord’s notice and an LTB eviction order are different documents. A notice is not, by itself, an enforceable eviction order. The grounds, required process, and available response depend on the circumstances.

Different forms address different situations. An N4 relates to non-payment of rent; other notices, including N12 and N13 notices, have different purposes and requirements. Avoid treating all notices as interchangeable or assuming that the date on one document answers every question about your tenancy.

Keep the entire notice and record when and how it was delivered. If there is already an application or order, include that in the discussion.

## Build a useful record

Prepare your lease, notices, rent-payment records, relevant messages, and photographs of disputed conditions, if any. Put important events in date order. If you made a repair request or received a response, keep both.

Before sharing records, ask which documents the office needs and how to provide them. Keep your original documents and do not rely on an initial inquiry to meet a response or hearing requirement.
