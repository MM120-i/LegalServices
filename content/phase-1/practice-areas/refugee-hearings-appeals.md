# Refugee Hearings & Appeals

Route: `/practice-areas/refugee-hearings-appeals`

H1: Refugee hearing and appeal paralegal services in Toronto.

A refugee hearing or negative decision can bring difficult questions about evidence, preparation, and what comes next. You deserve a clear explanation of the process and an accurate understanding of who can represent you.

Abed Legal Services Professional Corporation lists refugee hearings and appeals among its practice areas. Contact the Scarborough office to ask about your Immigration and Refugee Board proceeding and the services available within permitted Ontario paralegal scope.

## Be clear about the proceeding

An IRB proceeding and an immigration application are not interchangeable services. Explain which organization sent your document and what stage your matter has reached. If you have received a decision, have both the decision and its reasons available.

The IRB permits eligible paid counsel, including qualifying provincial law-society members, to represent people at the Refugee Appeal Division. That does not mean every matter has an appeal available or that a representative accepts every kind of case.

If your next step may involve Federal Court, seek advice from a lawyer about that process. This page does not offer that representation.

## What to prepare

Have your hearing notice or decision, the date you received it, and a short list of any upcoming requirements. Tell the office if you already have a representative. Explain what language or accommodation support you need so arrangements can be discussed without assuming availability.

Do not include your detailed protection narrative, immigration identification numbers, or sensitive family information in the initial website inquiry. Ask how to share records through an appropriate channel.

## Proposed approach

1. Confirm the division, stage of proceedings, and any urgent dates.
2. Determine whether the matter fits the services Abed accepts.
3. Explain the proposed work, fees, and document needs.
4. If retained, prepare for the agreed proceeding and discuss its requirements with you.

## Questions

### Can you guarantee refugee protection or a successful appeal?

No. The decision rests with the relevant decision-maker. No result can be promised.

### Can every refused claim be appealed to the RAD?

Do not assume that an appeal is available. Eligibility and the applicable process require individual assessment.

### What if I received a decision recently?

Seek help promptly. Tell the office when and how you received the decision. Waiting for a website reply does not extend a filing deadline.
