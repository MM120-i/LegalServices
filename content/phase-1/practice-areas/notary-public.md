## Notarization and commissioning are different

A commissioner can administer an oath or affirmation and take an affidavit or declaration. A notary can also perform functions such as verifying signatures and certifying copies, subject to the applicable requirements.

Tell the office exactly what the receiving organization has requested. If its instructions are unclear, ask that organization to clarify rather than assuming the two services are interchangeable.

Ontario currently requires physical presence for notarization. Do not assume a video appointment will meet your document’s requirements.

## Before your appointment

Call with the type and number of documents, where they will be used, and any instructions from the receiving organization. Ask what identification and originals are required. Avoid signing in advance when the signature needs to be witnessed; confirm the instructions first.

Documents for use outside Canada can involve additional steps such as authentication or an apostille. Ask the receiving authority what it requires. This page does not promise that notarization alone makes a document acceptable everywhere or that Abead offers authentication services.
