# Employment & Human Rights Matters

Route: `/practice-areas/employment-human-rights-matters`

H1: Employment and human rights paralegal services in Toronto.

Losing a job, dealing with unpaid wages, or experiencing possible discrimination can leave you unsure which process to use. These concerns can involve different rights, decision-makers, and time limits.

An unpaid-wages concern, a dismissal and a discrimination complaint may follow different procedures. Tell the office what happened, when it happened and whether another proceeding is already underway so the available scope of assistance can be discussed.

## Choose the process carefully

A workplace disagreement is not automatically a human rights claim. The HRTO deals with matters under Ontario’s Human Rights Code, which connects protected grounds with areas such as employment, housing, and services.

Similarly, a termination concern is not always best addressed through the same route as an unpaid-wage complaint. The facts, employment relationship, amounts involved, and any existing proceeding can affect the available process.

Before filing multiple complaints or signing a release, get advice about your particular situation. A general webpage cannot determine the route that is right for you.

## What to prepare

Gather any employment agreement, termination letter, proposed release, pay records, and relevant communications. Create a short timeline of events, including the last day worked and the dates of any alleged incidents. If you belong to a union or have already started another process, mention that early.

For a human rights inquiry, explain briefly what happened and what connection you believe it has to a protected ground. Keep sensitive health and personal details out of the initial website form.

## Questions

### Is all unfair treatment discrimination under the Code?

No. The legal test requires more than a general feeling of unfairness. The facts and their connection to a protected ground and covered area need assessment.

### Can I bring a human rights issue outside the workplace?

The Code also covers other areas, including housing and services. Confirm whether Abead accepts your particular type of matter.

### Should I sign a severance document before calling?

If you want advice before signing, seek it promptly and explain any proposed deadline. This webpage cannot assess the document or determine your entitlements.
