### Send a consultation request

Keep your message brief. Please do not include identity numbers, medical information, detailed immigration history, financial account details, or other sensitive records. The office can discuss how to provide documents if needed.

Field labels and helpers:

- **Full name** — Required. “How should we address you?”
- **Phone number** — Required. “Best number to reach you.”
- **Email address** — Optional. “Provide an email if you would like a reply by email.”
- **What do you need help with?** — Required. Options: Traffic Tickets / Provincial Offences; Accident Benefits Claims; Refugee Hearings & Appeals; Landlord & Tenant Matters; Employment & Human Rights Matters; Small Claims Court Matters; Notary Public & Commissioning; Other / Not sure.

- Topic missing: “Please choose a service or select ‘Other / Not sure.’”
- Acknowledgement missing: “Please acknowledge the statement before sending.”
- General validation: “Please check the highlighted fields and try again.”
